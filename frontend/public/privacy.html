Privacy policy

Canopy separates private account verification from peer-blind interest rooms.

Account verification data

When you create an account, Canopy stores your email address, hashed password, full name, gender, and two-letter nationality code in a private authentication table. Those fields are only returned to your own authenticated session.

Peer-blind pods and channels

Interest matching never filters or groups users by gender or nationality. Inside every room, Canopy assigns a unique animal alias and a room-scoped peer hash. Messages, reactions, polls, and WebRTC signaling frames never include your real name, email, gender, nationality, or account ID.

Opt-outs and deletion

Leaving a room removes your membership and records a pod opt-out so future matches skip that room. You may request account deletion at any time.
